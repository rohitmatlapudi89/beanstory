    Why this recipe
    • 1000 MASL + Cultured Natural: airy cells, further weakened by microbes, shatter into fines. Grind coarser so the bed keeps flowing.
    • Agtron 65 + fermentation sugars: soluble compounds sit right at the surface. 90–91 °C acts as a brake against bitter, "medicinal" notes.
    • SL9 + Chandragiri: SL9's acids dissolve fast; Chandragiri's sugars and oils dissolve slowly. Pulse pours with waits hold water in the bed longer.
    • Fast filter: the tabbed Hario 100W drains in 10–12 s, so the pauses do the work instead of the paper.
    If the cup tastes…
    Sour, thin, wateryGrind finer, raise the water temperature, or swirl more.
    Bitter, dry, hollowGrind coarser, drop 2 °C, or pour more gently.
